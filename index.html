Frequently asked questions

How do I join the server?

Open Minecraft Bedrock, choose Play → Servers → Add Server, then enter the IP address and port shown above.

Can I join from mobile, console or Windows?

The server is intended for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Platform availability and console connection steps may vary.

Why does the server show offline?

The server may be offline, restarting, under maintenance, or the status provider may be temporarily unavailable. Try refreshing after a moment.
